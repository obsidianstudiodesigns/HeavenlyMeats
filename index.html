Heavenly Meats started in 2023 with one idea: good meat shouldn't cost a fortune. Veronica runs the counter on Logan Street, serving families, farm workers, braai masters and anyone passing through on the N1.

We keep it simple. Honest cuts, fair weights, fresh and frozen on hand, and a phone that gets answered.
