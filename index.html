Good to know

Where is Heavenly Meats?

We're at 41 Logan Street, Touwsrivier, in the Western Cape. It's an easy stop if you're travelling the N1 between Cape Town and the Karoo.

Is Heavenly Meats a butcher or a wholesaler?

Both. You can buy a few chops for tonight's braai or order in bulk for a farm, function or freezer. Send your list and we'll quote you.

Do you sell fresh or frozen meat?

Both. We stock fresh and frozen beef, lamb and mutton, pork, chicken, Ouma's boerewors and, when available, game. Stock changes week to week, so ask what's in.

How do I place an order?

Call or WhatsApp Veronica on 076 977 5683 with what you need. We'll confirm prices and availability and have it ready for you to collect.

Do you serve customers outside Touwsrivier?

Yes. Customers from De Doorns, Laingsburg, Matjiesfontein, Worcester and the surrounding farms are welcome. Order ahead and collect on Logan Street.
